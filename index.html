  • Always pay through our official system (Rex Bank).
  • Do not pay outside the platform to avoid the 10% deduction fee. If you pay privately and get scammed, we cannot help or refund you.

⏳ 2. Reviews & Closing Tickets
  • You have 3 days to check your delivery and report any issues.
  • If the work is good, close the order by signing ✅. If there is a problem, Reject and tell us why.

What is Escrow?
✕

It works the same way as Rex Bank: escrow is a secure hold on your payment. When you hire an editor, your money doesn't go to them right away — it's held safely by Rex Edit Hub first.

Once you review the delivery and sign off, the held funds are released to the editor. If something goes wrong instead — the editor doesn't deliver, or the work is rejected — your money is returned quickly rather than being stuck with someone you can't trust yet.

In short: escrow protects both sides — the editor knows the client has actually paid, and the client knows their money is safe until the work is approved.
